## What to do now that AI Overviews turned search into reading sessions
- Source: https://searchengineland.com/ai-overviews-search-reading-sessions-479244
- Author: Kevin Indig — Published: 2026-06-03
- Why it matters: large-sample original data on how AI Overviews change clicks — the demand-side reality any AI-content playbook has to design around.

Key takeaways:
- **Intent compression:** across all 5 intent types, ~42–48.5% of users now stay on the SERP past 21s (vs. a previous 12–32% spread) — AI Overviews flatten behavior across intents. (n ≈ 846k U.S. sessions.)
- **The "second impression" decides clicks:** optimize metadata per page type — product (review counts, rating schema), category (ItemList schema), blog (visible dates, author entity links).
- **Shift from intent-prediction to SERP positioning:** how your listing looks *against* the AI answer matters more than predicting dwell.
- **Verification burden moves to the answer engine**, not the user clicking through.
- **Monitor branded prompts to LLMs** — treat it like defensive brand-keyword bidding.
- Context: 1.5B+ people now use AI Overviews.

> "The AIO erased that tell… Both slow down, both stay, both behave alike on the SERP page."
