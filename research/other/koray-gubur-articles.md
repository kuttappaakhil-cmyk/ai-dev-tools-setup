## Koray's Agents: Generative AI Agents for Semantic SEO & Topical Authority
- Source: https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a
- Published: 2024-06-29
- Why it matters: the planning *architecture* beneath high-quality AI content production — how to use AI agents for semantic structure, not just drafting.

Key concepts:
- **Topical maps** — model entity relationships and content networks, not keyword lists.
- **Semantic content networks** — interlinked pages covering related concepts with contextual relevance.
- **Query gap analysis** — find competitor weaknesses via intent patterns and SERP-feature opportunities.
- **Entity processing** — systematic placement/optimization of named entities in context.
- **Aspect-based sentiment summarization** — aggregate reviews by feature importance + tone.
- **Outranking cost mapping** — visualize competitor metrics to prioritize which networks to target.
- **Topicality scoring** — weigh meaningful vs. generic n-grams to judge contextual relevance.

Process: a library of **287+ specialized GPT agents** that perform NLP tasks while *explaining* their semantic reasoning — pushing past naive lemmatization toward end-to-end content strategy.

> "Creating a topical map is not merely listing keywords or entities."
