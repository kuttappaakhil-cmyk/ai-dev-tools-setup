## 2026-06-24 — AI Mode turns search into a recommendation engine
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_how-consumers-navigate-high-stakes-purchases-activity-7475547777897472001-Tax0
Opening: "Search is becoming a recommendation engine."
- AI Mode builds *shortlists* for high-stakes purchases — visibility now means being on the list, not ranking a page.
- Inclusion depends on perceived authority more than keyword relevance.
- 93% of consumers accept AI-generated shortlists; familiarity with options shifts acceptance.
- Reddit showed in only 2 of 149 AI Mode sessions vs ~19% in classic search — community plays don't transfer to recommendation engines.
> "Visibility is no longer just about being found, it's about being included."
Annotation: reframes the *goal* of AI-era content from ranking to being a trusted, included option — core to the playbook.
