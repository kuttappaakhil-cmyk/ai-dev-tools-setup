# Research Sources — AI-Powered SEO Content Production

> Topic: **AI-powered SEO content production** for B2B SaaS — how operators actually use AI to plan, produce, optimize, and distribute search content (and how they keep it ranking as search shifts to AI answers / GEO / AEO).

This file is the index of the 10 experts behind this research, why each was chosen, where their content lives, and what is being collected from each.

---

## How these 10 were selected

The brief is explicit that *10 high-signal sources beat 50 generic ones*, and that we should avoid "the first Google results." Selection criteria:

1. **Practitioners, not commentators.** Every person here *operates*: runs an agency/consultancy, builds a product, leads content at a real company, or runs their own production system — not people who only write think-pieces.
2. **Distinct facets.** The panel is deliberately spread across the full production pipeline — strategy → semantic planning → AI-assisted writing → optimization → quality/EEAT → distribution → measuring AI-search impact — so the material can support a *real playbook*, not 10 versions of the same take.
3. **Currently active (2024–2026).** Each has shipped content within the last ~12 months; recency was verified, not assumed.
4. **Range of stances on purpose.** Includes both AI-bullish operators (Goldie, Huang, Law) and skeptical/quality-first voices (Ray, Schwartz). A playbook built only on hype is fragile.
5. **Collectible.** Prioritized people with public YouTube/podcast/newsletter footprints so the content can actually be gathered through APIs/tools.
